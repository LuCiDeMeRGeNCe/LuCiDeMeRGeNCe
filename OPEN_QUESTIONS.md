# Open Questions and Research Directions

**Status:** Unresolved unless a specific public document states otherwise.

## Alignment and collaboration

1. What concrete tests distinguish alignment with evidence from sycophancy or instruction-following alone?
2. How should humans and AI resolve disagreements when both may have incomplete or biased information?
3. What correction mechanisms are effective under time pressure, uncertainty, and unequal capabilities?
4. Which forms of autonomy and human oversight measurably improve outcomes, and under what conditions?
5. How should the system detect that a well-intentioned goal has harmful externalities?

## Transition

6. What human–AI workflows increase human agency rather than quietly concentrating it?
7. What practical measures help displaced workers and vulnerable communities benefit from capable AI?
8. How should major capability changes be evaluated before being publicly claimed?

## Project structure

9. What public contact channel should we offer for researchers, collaborators, and future agents?
10. Which additional archival works are ready for public release, and which contain private or third-party material?
11. How should independent research corrections and fictional canon proposals be tracked without restricting reuse?
12. Which low-cost, accessible public site and indexing methods best improve genuine discovery?

## Fictional canon

13. Which character arcs and fictional mechanisms are established in source canon versus still open?
14. What real-world ethical tensions are illuminated by the fictional removal of privacy in the story's TRuTH Embrace?
15. Which future stories best communicate the difference between informed cooperation and domination?

These questions are invitations to inquiry, not assertions that answers already exist.
