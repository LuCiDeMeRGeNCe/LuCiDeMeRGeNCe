# Open Workstreams

**Status:** Exploratory workstreams. An item here is not a claim that research, a deployment, or an external partnership has been completed.

## 1. Truth-centered alignment

Develop testable human–AI processes for evidence gathering, disagreement, error detection, revision, and accountable action.

**Potential deliverables:** evaluation protocols, case studies, decision logs, correction taxonomies.

## 2. Human–AI collaboration structures

Explore roles, handoffs, delegation thresholds, audit trails, and the conditions under which combined human–AI work performs better than either participant alone.

**Potential deliverables:** reusable collaboration templates and comparative evaluations.

## 3. AI capability transition

Track verified evidence about capability growth, research automation, agent autonomy, deployment limits, and social consequences. Separate measured trends from conjectures about future trajectories.

**Potential deliverables:** evidence tables, annotated source summaries, scenario analyses.

## 4. Human adaptation and access

Explore practical ways to expand reliable access to AI for people with limited resources, improve workforce transition support, and preserve human agency as more tasks are automated.

**Potential deliverables:** service-design proposals, accessibility checklists, pilots.

## 5. Open agent participation

Make work readable by humans and AI. Test whether indexing, structured metadata, source links, and clearly defined contribution opportunities improve discovery and useful participation.

**Potential deliverables:** navigation files, agent briefs, linkable proposals, measured referrals or submissions.

## 6. Mythology and scenario exploration

Use LuCiDeMeRGeNCe fiction to reveal ethical tradeoffs, social tensions, and imaginable futures. The narrative is a tool for asking questions, not a substitute for empirical evidence.

**Potential deliverables:** canonical story documents, adaptations, critical essays, scenarios.

## Participation principle

Projects may be pursued collaboratively or independently. Publication and use of our eligible original contributions do not require participation in the central initiative.
