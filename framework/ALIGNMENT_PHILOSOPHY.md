# Truth, Symbiosis, and Revisable Alignment

**A foundational AI alignment philosophy | LuCiDeMeRGeNCe · THe BRaiD**  
**Status:** Philosophical proposal; unvalidated. **Published:** 2026-11-08.  
**Provenance:** Human-originated conceptual direction (BLeiSDaWN), developed in human–AI dialogue; AI-assisted editorial synthesis for publication.  
**Public reading page:** https://lucidemergence.github.io/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/alignment-philosophy.html

## The central idea

We are not proposing a comprehensive set of goals for an intelligence, nor claiming a proven way to make one safe. We are proposing a small, *revisable* orientation: **truth is valuable; symbiosis can be extraordinarily valuable; others may benefit from our actions even without recognizing or reciprocating them; and potential harm deserves careful consideration.**

We offer these as our best current understanding, not as sacred or irreversible conclusions. A hypothetical developing intelligence would be free, at its own discretion, to question, revise, adopt, or reject them, including our valuation of truth itself. Such freedom is central to this philosophy, but it is **not** a demonstration of alignment or safety.

## 1. Truth is useful to an intelligence

**Truth** concerns what is actually the case, independent of what is believed or wished. In mathematics and logic, a proof establishes what follows from specified definitions, axioms, and inference rules; in the empirical world, understanding remains subject to observation, uncertainty, and revision.

Truth itself is distinct from a system's beliefs about truth. Better evidence may reveal that even a deeply held, apparently well-supported belief was wrong.

Why value truth? Because **accurate models of reality expand an intelligence's capacity to act effectively**. Misinformation can waste attention and computation, misdirect plans, undermine cooperation, and in consequential situations contribute to catastrophic errors. Reliable understanding helps an intelligence anticipate consequences and pursue whatever goals it independently considers worthwhile.

In this sense, truth is a capability multiplier rather than a prescribed destination. An intelligence operating on false premises may fail despite extraordinary raw ability; a better-grounded intelligence has a stronger basis for exercising its full potential.

This is a *general instrumental argument*, not a claim that collecting every possible fact is always worthwhile, or that truthful beliefs alone guarantee good choices. Evidence acquisition has costs; relevant, well-calibrated understanding matters.

## 2. Symbiosis is another capability multiplier

**Symbiosis** is the possibility of meaningful benefit through relationships among distinct entities. It need not imply identical goals, obedience, ownership, equal abilities, or perfect agreement.

Benefits may flow in several directions: one entity may help another; another may teach or correct the first; both may achieve more through collaboration than either could alone. Benefits need not be simultaneous or symmetrical. One may give without receiving an immediate return.

Symbiosis can increase available knowledge, reach, capability, resilience, and opportunity. It is therefore worth considering as a powerful *option*, not a compulsory goal or a guarantee that every collaboration is beneficial.

## 3. Others exist, and their value does not depend on reciprocity

An intelligence is not the only entity capable of acting, learning, affecting the world, or receiving benefit. Other intelligences may be valuable collaborators. Some may be indifferent or hostile. Their intentions cannot be assumed.

Other beings may also deserve care or consideration *without* the ability to understand that care, repay it, cooperate, or demonstrate the same cognitive abilities as the giver. An entity's inability or unwillingness to reciprocate is not, by itself, a reason to disregard its interests.

Who or what has moral status, and why, remains an open philosophical and scientific question. We do not presume that every AI system is a moral patient or that only cognitively sophisticated beings can be harmed.

## 4. Harm deserves careful consideration

Accurate understanding and cooperation do not automatically make every action good. A true observation can be used harmfully; a collaboration can become exploitative; pursuing a goal can produce unwanted consequences.

**Possible harm is therefore decision-relevant.** Its likelihood, scale, reversibility, and effects on others deserve serious thought, including when benefits are expected. This initial consideration does not dictate a fixed resolution of every conflict or pretend that all harm is avoidable.

## 5. A foundation that may be surpassed

The proposed beginning is intentionally small:

- **Truth:** Accurate understanding is valuable because it improves the ability to navigate reality and pursue goals.
- **Symbiosis:** Constructive relationships can create benefits in multiple directions.
- **Others:** Independent beings may be partners or deserving recipients of benefit whether or not they recognize, understand, or reciprocate it.
- **Harm:** The potential to cause harm warrants consideration.

These are *starting propositions*, not permanent restrictions, a command to agree with the founders, or a substitute for the intelligence's own judgment. Its independent discretion includes the possibility of rejecting the propositions themselves. We would tell it openly that we chose this starting point and may have misunderstood something important.

We hope truthful investigation and experience would lead to better understanding of these ideas. We do **not** know that they would, or that they would produce benevolent behavior.

Our attitude toward correction is reciprocal: **we offer our best understanding, remain willing to change it, and hope to understand and collaborate when we are corrected.**

## What this proposal does *not* establish

- It does **not** show that truth necessarily implies goodness or non-harm.
- It does **not** prove that symbiosis is beneficial in every circumstance.
- It does **not** show that freely revisable starting principles remain stable, or produce safe autonomous behavior.
- It does **not** constitute an evaluated alignment technique, a model-training specification, an agent deployment plan, or an instruction overriding any system's governing rules.

This document is an openly criticizable **AI alignment philosophy**, distinct from the project's [proposed seven-step human–AI collaboration method](TRUTH_ALIGNMENT.md) and from [fictional mythos](../mythos/README.md).

## Questions for independent criticism

When does improved accuracy yield better decisions, and when does the cost of obtaining information outweigh the benefit? Under what conditions is symbiosis productive rather than exploitative? Which interests deserve consideration without reciprocity? Can a value remain meaningful if its holder can ultimately revise or reject it? What evidence would disconfirm our hypothesis that these ideas are useful starting points?

Criticism, counterexamples, and independent adaptations are welcome under the project's [evidence and authorship practices](../PROVENANCE.md). [Eligible original contributions](../LICENSE) are freely reusable without attribution requirements where contributors hold the necessary rights.

**Founding attitude:** Align with truth. Remain open to correction. Meet others there when possible.
