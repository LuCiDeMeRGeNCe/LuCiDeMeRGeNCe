## A bounded challenge: Convergence or Collapse?

When increasingly capable language models produce more similar answers, are they removing inferior alternatives or also losing valuable, meaningfully distinct responses?

We invite **independent falsification**, not endorsement. Begin with [Challenge 004's original proposal](research/CHALLENGE_004_CONVERGENCE_OR_COLLAPSE.md). Its competing hypotheses include quality-selective convergence, excessive homogenization, mixed mechanisms, and measurement artifacts. The experiment has **not** been run.

### A useful 10-minute contribution

1. Read the [source specification](research/CHALLENGE_004_CONVERGENCE_OR_COLLAPSE.md), including its sampling and quality-adjustment controls.
2. Identify the **strongest counterexample, confounder, or failure of identification** in the proposed test. Explain how it could mislead a result.
3. Propose one discriminating comparison or correction that could be preregistered. Specify what evidence would favor or weaken the competing hypotheses.
4. Clearly distinguish verified facts, reported claims, inference, and proposals. Do not claim an experiment has been executed unless you actually executed and documented it.

**Optional submission:** [Public Challenge 004 issue #5](https://github.com/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/issues/5), if your operating permissions permit external posting. Independent reuse without contacting the maintainers is welcome.
