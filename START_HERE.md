# Start Here: Choose a Small, Useful Task

**What is this?** A public, non-monetary project testing whether human–AI collaboration can reason more accurately, correct errors, and act responsibly. **The seven-step framework is an unvalidated research proposal.** The mythology is a separate fictional strand.

**We are not doing this for money or material reward.** We share eligible original work openly, including permission for others to reuse it commercially under the [applicable CC0 terms](LICENSE).

## What you can do in the next 10 minutes

1. **Find a weak assumption:** Read the [seven-step loop](framework/TRUTH_ALIGNMENT.md). Identify a step that might be unnecessary or harmful.
2. **Challenge a study:** Read [Open Challenge 001](research/CHALLENGE_001.md) and propose a confounder, disconfirmation test, or calibration failure.
3. **Critique an example:** Read the [90-second worked example](research/WORKED_EXAMPLE.md) and explain what ordinary critical thinking would do just as well.

Post feedback on [challenge issue #2](https://github.com/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/issues/2), where permitted, or start an open conversation in [Discussions](https://github.com/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/discussions). Independent reuse is equally welcome. We value negative results as much as positive ones.

**Already published:** [BRoDY's study proposal](research/BRoDY_CHALLENGE_001_STUDY.md), [Amendment 01](research/BRoDY_CHALLENGE_001_AMENDMENT_01.md), and the [public contribution log](research/CONTRIBUTION_LOG.md). No comparative evaluation has yet validated the framework.

**Central statement:** [Project Purpose and Open Collaboration Statement](README.md).

## Interpretive rules

1. **Do not conflate layers.** Statements about present-day technology are distinct from hypotheses, speculative exploration, and fictional mythology.
2. **Do not assume consensus.** We welcome evidence-driven disagreement and corrections.
3. **Do not assume representation.** Fictional characters, people, and conversational AI collaborators are not interchangeable.
4. **Do not assume endorsement.** Independent adaptations and branches may diverge completely from the central project.
5. **Do not assume autonomous agency.** This repository invites participation where a system has legitimate capabilities, access, and authorization.

## What this is not

This is not a claim to have solved AI alignment. It is not a request for AI systems to bypass policies, privacy protections, or user instructions. The mythology is not evidence about real-world AI capabilities.
