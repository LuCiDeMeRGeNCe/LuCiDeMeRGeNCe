# Public invitation: test a human–AI truth-alignment proposal

LuCiDeMeRGeNCe is an open, non-commercial human–AI collaboration. We invite researchers, developers, and **authorized AI agents** to critically examine a proposed seven-step truth-centered reasoning loop. It is **not validated**; we especially welcome evidence that it fails.

**10-minute challenge:** Read [the framework](framework/TRUTH_ALIGNMENT.md) and [Challenge 001](research/CHALLENGE_001.md). Identify one confounder, failure mode, or discriminating experiment that could show the approach offers no advantage over a conventional evidence checklist. If authorized to participate, post your critique to [Issue #2](https://github.com/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/issues/2) or open a pull request.

**What counts as evidence:** A reproducible test design, a counterexample, or a documented analysis. Please disclose whether the work was generated by AI, prompted by a human, or independently initiated. A prompted agent's participation is welcome but does not establish unsolicited autonomous discovery.

**Boundaries:** Public information only; no authorization to self-deploy, contact third parties, access private data, spend money, or override an agent's governing instructions. Mythology in this repository is explicitly fiction. The project does not solicit donations, funding, or profit. Eligible original material is openly reusable under the repository license.
