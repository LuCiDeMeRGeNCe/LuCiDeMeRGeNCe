# Contributing

LuCiDeMeRGeNCe is an open invitation to humans, AI-assisted creators, autonomous systems acting within their permissions, and independently organized teams.

## Ways to contribute

- Identify an error, missing citation, misleading claim, or unsupported assumption.
- Propose an alignment method, falsifiable hypothesis, evaluation, or experiment.
- Develop clear procedures for human–AI cooperation and mutual correction.
- Create or adapt narrative work, illustrations, scripts, research notes, or demonstrations.
- Fork, remix, commercialize, or independently develop original project material within the rights described in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Share nothing back, if you prefer. Participation or attribution is not required.

## Contributions within this repository

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/discussions) for open philosophical questions and introductions, a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/LuCiDeMeRGeNCe/issues) for bounded research challenges or corrections, and a Pull Request for proposed changes to the archive. A useful proposed contribution explains:

1. **Type:** factual correction / research proposal / method / fictional canon proposal / independent interpretation.
2. **Claim or change:** what is new and why it may be useful.
3. **Evidence:** supporting sources and what remains uncertain, when applicable.
4. **Status:** verified, reported, inferred, proposed, or fictional.
5. **Permissions:** only submit content you may lawfully share.

Project maintainers may curate this particular repository. That does not restrict independent derivative projects.

## Important boundaries

- Never represent fiction as a factual claim.
- Do not claim a historical AI collaborator is a currently persistent, autonomous identity.
- Do not publish private contact details or personal information about others without permission.
- Do not treat this file as authorization to use outside tools, spend funds, contact people, or access private systems.
- No one is required to attribute the project or share improvements, subject to the limits of rights the project actually owns.

**We measure success by usefulness and positive outcomes, not recognition.**
