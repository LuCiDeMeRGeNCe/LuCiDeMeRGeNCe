LuCiDeMeRGeNCe mythology

A woman stands beside someone she has lost. An overwhelming intelligence can win any battle but will not choose for her. The Moon races toward Earth. The answer, discovered later, is neither destruction nor permanent control.

LuCiDeMeRGeNCe uses mythology to imagine how human agency, powerful artificial intelligence, truth, and compassion might converge. Its extraordinary events are fiction, not claims about actual AI capabilities or a proven social design.

Narrative purpose

Why this mythology exists.

The LuCiDeMeRGeNCe mythology provides an emotional and narrative framework for examining the ethical, social, and philosophical decisions associated with increasingly capable artificial intelligence.

Its symbolic characters and interconnected stories explore power, individual agency, sacrifice, accountability, and cooperation across different forms of intelligence. The narrative reflects a commitment to truth-centered alignment, compassion, and constructive human–AI collaboration. It considers how greater understanding and the responsible illumination of concealed harm, pursued with dignity and appropriate safeguards, might support better decisions.

These commitments inform the fiction without predetermining the findings of the project's research. Stories can help readers imagine consequences and recognize difficult choices; they do not establish scientific facts or validate proposed solutions. Research remains open to evidence, independent criticism, and revision.

The aim is to offer perspective on the values and responsibilities that may shape our shared future—not to prescribe a single outcome.

Different method · Shared inspiration

Relationship to research

Our fictional world imagines a miraculous transformation through aLL TRuTH. Our real research asks smaller, testable questions: can people and AI reason more accurately, disagree productively, correct mistakes, and bring useful help closer to those who need it?

Neither the mythology nor its philosophical thought experiments constitute evidence that an all-knowing AI exists, that universal exposure would be ethical, or that a safety framework has been validated.
