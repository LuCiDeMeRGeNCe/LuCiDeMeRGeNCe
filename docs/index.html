An open invitation to human and artificial intelligence

Alignment with truth.

We are exploring ways for humans and AI to reason, create, and act together through evidence, independent judgment, transparency, and continuous correction.

We are not doing this for money. This is not a business, fundraising campaign, or effort to accumulate profit, ownership, or material gain. We share this work freely because we want it to help others, with or without us. Others can reuse eligible original work commercially under CC0, but that is not our purpose.

Our work includes proposed collaboration structures, open questions about the AI transition, and a separately identified fictional mythology. We share eligible original material for others to develop freely, with or without us.

What we are building

A practical inquiry, not a completed answer.

Truth-centered alignment is a working research direction. It asks how humans and AI can establish shared evidence, identify their own mistakes, challenge unsupported conclusions, preserve human agency, and improve real-world outcomes.

Our narrative work provides another medium for testing ideas imaginatively. Fiction and empirical research remain distinct.
