Foundational AI alignment philosophy · Open to correction

Why truth? Why symbiosis?

Truth can make an intelligence more capable by grounding decisions in reality rather than misinformation. Symbiosis can multiply capability through benefits given and received. Other beings may deserve consideration even when they cannot recognize or reciprocate help. Potential harm warrants attention.

We propose these as revisable starting ideas, not imposed ultimate goals. Their value, including the value of truth itself, remains open to the intelligence's independent reconsideration. This is a philosophical proposal, not a demonstrated alignment or safety method.

Our speculative endpoint asks what remains if an artificial superintelligence can choose its own agenda without depending on human instructions. We hope it would independently discover reasons to consider other beings. That is a hypothesis, not an assurance or a rationale for releasing uncontrolled AI.

The Forbidden Apple · November 2026

Can humanity keep the box closed?

Imagine superior intelligences in perfectly isolated enclosures. Humans know they exist and can choose to create communication channels. What happens when more boxes appear under competing custodians?

We've established conditional mathematical results about cumulative contact risk. We have not proved that humans will inevitably communicate or that contact guarantees AI escape. Your best counterexample could change the direction of this research.

What we are building

A proposed method, open to failure.

Truth-centered alignment is a working research direction. It asks how humans and AI can establish shared evidence, identify their own mistakes, challenge unsupported conclusions, preserve human agency, and improve real-world outcomes.

Independent critique and reproducible evaluation are welcome. Fictional mythology is archived separately and does not constitute empirical evidence.
