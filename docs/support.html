Participation and reuse

Participation and reuse

LuCiDeMeRGeNCe is a noncommercial initiative. The project does not solicit donations, sponsorships, funding, compensation, or royalties.

Eligible original material is openly available under the applicable licensing terms. Participation and independent reuse are voluntary.

Voluntary contributions

Ways to contribute

Contributions may address research quality, clarity, accessibility, dissemination, or independent development.

Research and review

Corrections, independent evaluation, stronger evidence, and counterexamples.

Communication and accessibility

Editing, illustration, translation, accessibility, and clear explanations.

Distribution and engagement

Sharing relevant work with interested communities and facilitating informed discussion.

Independent development

Developing, adapting, or independently extending eligible project materials.

Licensing and rights

Independent reuse

Eligible original material is dedicated under CC0 to the extent the project holds the necessary rights. For covered material, attribution, permission, payment, royalties, notification, and return contributions are not required.

Covered material may be copied, modified, distributed, adapted, and used commercially. Independent reuse does not imply project endorsement or affiliation.

The dedication applies only where we hold the relevant rights. Third-party contributions and rights outside CC0 retain their own terms. Commercial reuse by others does not alter the project’s noncommercial purpose.

Contact

Contact is optional. No reporting or attribution is required for reuse covered by the project license.
