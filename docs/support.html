Our approach to support

We ask for nothing.
We welcome what helps the work.

LuCiDeMeRGeNCe and THe BRaiD are not seeking money, funding, sponsorships, donations, royalties, recognition, or personal financial gain.

Our work is freely offered. It isn't a business or a fundraising campaign.

So why write about support at all? Because if someone can clarify an idea, improve the evidence, carry a message to new people, or finish something useful, refusing that possibility would serve no one.

Independence matters. Isolation is not the goal.

Freely offered, never expected

What we would welcome.

Not support for personal gain. Support for the questions, the work, and its ability to be understood.

Sharper understanding

Corrections, independent criticism, stronger research, counterexamples, and ideas that change our minds.

Clearer expression

Writing, editing, illustration, translation, accessibility, and new ways of making complex ideas approachable.

Wider reach

Sharing, introductions, communities, conversations, and opportunities for a useful thought to find interested people.

Further development

Take a fragment and finish it, adapt a concept, or build something together. You can also continue the work completely independently.

We would be delighted if someone carried an idea farther than we ever could, whether with us or without us.

Open work, no ownership toll

No credit or royalty required.

We release eligible original textual contributions under CC0 to the extent we have the rights to do so. For those contributions, attribution, permission, payment, royalties, notification, and return contributions are not required.

You may use, modify, expand, complete, distribute, or independently develop that material, including commercially. If you build something useful, we don't seek a share of its proceeds or an ongoing place in its story.

The dedication applies only where we hold the relevant rights. Third-party contributions and rights outside CC0 retain their own terms. Independent commercial uses do not make this a commercial project for us.

Why say any of this?

Because the purpose matters more than the source.

Writing down our position can feel unnecessary. Our conduct should speak for itself. But we would be foolish to close the door on help that could improve learning, expression, or reach.

We do not assume our ideas are correct or uniquely valuable. We want them examined, corrected where needed, and developed wherever they prove useful.

If the work grows beyond us, that's not a loss. It's a possibility we welcome.

No obligation, just an open door

Conversation is welcome.

For an introduction, question, idea, or possible collaboration, the public project email is bleisdawn@icloud.com. You can also join an open conversation through GitHub Discussions.

You don't need to contact us, credit us, or tell us how you used eligible work. We ask only that any statements presented as factual be judged on evidence, not on our enthusiasm for them.
