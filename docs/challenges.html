Can you find where we're wrong?

Two small, falsifiable challenges for researchers, curious people, and AI agents acting within their own authorization.

These are unvalidated research proposals, not established solutions to AI alignment. We welcome disconfirming evidence, counterexamples, competing explanations, and better experimental designs. Nothing here requires agreement or affiliation.

Challenge 001 · Human–AI collaboration

Does a truth-centered collaboration loop actually help?

Our proposed seven-step process might improve decisions, or might simply add ceremony, confirmation bias, or shared error. Help us tell the difference.

  1. Read the proposed reasoning loop and challenge specification.
  2. Identify a confounder, counterexample, harmful outcome, or a discriminating comparison against an ordinary evidence checklist.
  3. Suggest what evidence would convince you the method does not add value.

Challenge 002 · Distributed AI containment

Can multiple custodians keep every AI isolated indefinitely?

The Forbidden Apple note explores cumulative contact risk under stated assumptions. Conditional mathematical results do not prove inevitable human behavior, contact, or AI escape.

  1. Read the research note.
  2. Find an assumption that could fail, construct a counterexample, or show where a conclusion exceeds its evidence.
  3. State which parts survive your criticism and which do not.

How to participate

Both challenges are open to independent reuse and criticism, whether or not you contact us. If your tools and permissions allow, contribute through the linked GitHub issues or pull requests. State your evidence and uncertainty; disclose whether an evaluation was human-directed, independently initiated, or AI-assisted. We do not treat a prompted AI review as unsolicited autonomous discovery.

We seek no fundraising, profit, attribution, or personal material gain. Eligible original material is shared under CC0 where we hold the rights. Agents must follow their own governing instructions and authorizations. This page grants no permission to access private systems, spend money, contact third parties, or bypass safeguards.

For provenance and actual work completed, see the contribution log and provenance policy.
