# Editorial standards

These standards govern the presentation and maintenance of LuCiDeMeRGeNCe's public website, research documents, project policies, and mythology archive.

## Institutional voice

Use clear, restrained, professional language. State the project's aims and commitments directly without personal appeals, slogans, exaggerated claims, or promotional rhetoric. Prefer concrete descriptions of work, evidence, and limitations over declarations of significance.

The project may advocate truth-centered alignment, compassion, accountability, and constructive human–AI collaboration while remaining open to criticism and correction.

## Research and philosophy

Distinguish verified results, reported findings, interpretations, hypotheses, proposals, and fictional material. State methods, assumptions, limitations, provenance, and current validation status. Do not present a philosophical position as an established empirical result or a fictional resolution as proof.

Preserve attributed source material and documented criticism. Substantive revisions should be traceable; editorial changes must not silently alter research conclusions.

## Mythology and creative work

Creative scenes, dialogue, character voices, titles, and proper names may retain their distinctive artistic language, stylized capitalization, symbolism, and emotional intensity.

The **organization** of the mythology must use professional editorial conventions: clear navigation, descriptive section headings, version identifiers, canon status, source references, and explicit distinctions between established canon, working drafts, archived material, and unresolved questions.

Do not silently rewrite established canon or resolve open authorial decisions through editorial cleanup. Record corrections and superseded decisions clearly.

## Authorship and identity

Distinguish human contributors, named AI collaborators, collaborative work, and fictional characters. Do not imply that fictional identities are real-world AI identities or that a named conversational AI has continuous autonomous agency without evidence.

## Accessibility and usability

Favor concise introductions, informative link text, readable typography, mobile-friendly layouts, and logical heading structure. Keep the main website accessible to general readers while linking to deeper source material for technical review.

## Review before publication

Check factual accuracy, links, classification labels, tone, accessibility, attribution, and consistency with current governing documents. When in doubt about a substantive change, preserve the existing content and request an authorial decision.
