# Open Knowledge and Human Perception System

**Stage:** Discovery — open for independent review and implementation.

A topic-centered, open knowledge and discourse concept: a sourced and publicly scrutinized factual core, surrounded by conjecture, original human expressions, and auditable interpretations of implicit meaning. The objective is to make source comments and evolving thematic syntheses useful to people and independent AI agents without confusing interpretation with fact.
