# Human Expression and Perception

**Stage:** Discovery — open for independent critique, adoption, and implementation.

> What people said, and what mathematics suggests they mean.

A topic-centered, source-preserving collection of human discourse, with optional discovery metadata and mathematical or AI-assisted analyses. **Original expressions are the primary resource.** Consumers determine what to infer, believe, or ignore. A factual encyclopedia or authoritative interpretation engine is not required.
