# Support and reuse policy

LuCiDeMeRGeNCe is a noncommercial, non-fundraising initiative. It does not solicit donations, sponsorships, compensation, royalties, or personal financial support.

## Voluntary participation

The project welcomes voluntary contributions that advance its public work, including:

- Evidence-based criticism, corrections, and independent evaluation.
- Editorial improvements, translations, accessibility work, and explanatory materials.
- Research proposals, creative contributions, and practical demonstrations.
- Introductions to relevant communities and independent continuation of useful work.

Participation is optional. No contribution, affiliation, or response is required.

## Licensing and independent reuse

The [project license](LICENSE) dedicates eligible original material to the public domain under CC0 to the extent that the project holds the necessary rights. Such material may be copied, modified, redistributed, and used commercially without attribution, permission, payment, or a requirement to share subsequent work.

The project does not claim rights it does not possess. Third-party contributions and materials outside the applicable dedication remain subject to their respective terms.

Independent reuse is welcome and does not imply project endorsement or affiliation.
