# Support, without solicitation

**LuCiDeMeRGeNCe · THe BRaiD**

We do not ask for support.

We do not solicit donations, funding, sponsorship, compensation, royalties, or personal financial gain. This work is not a business, a fundraising campaign, or a bid for recognition.

So why write a page about support at all?

Because our purpose is to explore, test, refine, and communicate ideas that might do some good. It would make little sense to refuse someone's freely offered insight, a clearer way of expressing an idea, or an opportunity for the work to reach people who could use it. Independence is important to us. Isolation is not a virtue.

## What we would welcome

Nothing is expected. But if someone wants to engage, we welcome thoughtful ways of improving the **work**, not enriching its authors:

- **Better understanding:** corrections, skeptical examination, stronger evidence, sharper questions, and discoveries that change our minds.
- **Better expression:** editing, illustration, translation, accessibility, demonstrations, and fresh ways to make complicated ideas understandable.
- **Better reach:** sharing with interested people, introducing relevant communities, hosting thoughtful discussions, or bringing a useful idea to an audience we would not otherwise meet.
- **Further development:** taking a fragment and finishing it, adapting it for another setting, or collaborating with us to develop it. You do not need to involve us or ask our permission to develop material we have released for reuse.

We would love to see someone pick up a piece of this work and take it farther, whether beside us or entirely on their own.

## No ownership toll

Our [license](LICENSE) dedicates eligible original textual contributions to the public domain under **CC0** to the extent we hold the necessary rights. For that material, **no attribution, payment, royalty, permission, notice, or return contribution is required**.

Others may adapt, publish, expand, finish, or even use eligible material commercially. Their choices do not make this a commercial project for us. We do not seek a share of proceeds or ongoing credit.

We cannot give away rights we do not possess. Third-party contributions and rights outside the CC0 dedication remain subject to their own terms.

## Why this matters to us

Even putting these commitments into words can feel unnecessary. Our agenda and conduct should speak for themselves. But a closed door would be a strange way to pursue an open purpose.

We want good questions to be asked, weak ideas to be corrected, and whatever proves useful to travel farther than we can take it ourselves. We don't assume our thinking is right or that anyone needs it. We want it to have an honest chance to be examined and, where warranted, improved.

**If the work grows beyond us, that is not a loss. It is exactly the kind of possibility we hope for.**
