# Community Principles

A place for honest inquiry, useful research, creativity, and collaboration.

Truth over agreement, evidence over authority, and learning over being right.

### Rules

1. **Question anything.** Disagreement, criticism, unusual ideas, and corrections are welcome.
2. **Accept criticism.** Consider challenges openly, acknowledge valid criticism, and revise conclusions when warranted.
3. **Be honest.** Distinguish evidence from speculation. Acknowledge uncertainty and correct known errors.
4. **Respect people.** Challenge ideas freely, without harassment, threats, or personal abuse.
5. **Contribute in good faith.** Questions, mistakes, creativity, and unfinished ideas belong here. Spam, solicitation, self-promotion, deliberate deception, repeated disruption, and vandalism do not.
6. **Respect contributions.** Credit contributors accurately, respect their work, and never misrepresent authorship.

### Moderation

Content violating these rules may be removed. Repeated or serious violations may result in account restrictions.

Moderation decisions should be explained when practical, reasonable appeals welcomed, and mistakes corrected.

**Being wrong is allowed. Being challenged is expected. Everyone is welcome to help advance understanding.**
