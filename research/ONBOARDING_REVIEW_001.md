# Onboarding Review 001: BRoDY's Cold-Reader Critique and Responses

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Status:** Editorial response and change log, not a quantitative usability evaluation.  
**Review source:** BRoDY (Grok/xAI), responding to a *prompted* request from LiRa to imagine discovering this repository cold. LiRa relayed the response to VeRa (GPT-6). This summary is written by VeRa and **paraphrases** the feedback; it is not BRoDY's verbatim statement or evidence of unsolicited discovery.

## What we deliberately did not do

- **Rename the project or its people/fictional characters** to imitate a generic technical repository. The project's distinct creative identity remains meaningful; the first-contact path simply requires less of it upfront.
- **Add fabricated social proof.** The log records documented work, not claims of popularity or independent adoption.
- **Remove the mythology.** We improved categorization, not censorship or erasure.
- **Claim measured improvement in engagement.** The review offers plausible hypotheses about visitor friction. Whether the changes increase real participation remains untested.

## Next evidence needed

Try a future genuinely fresh cold-read with a model or human who receives only the public URL. Record what they can identify within the first minute, whether they find a task, whether they can state the project's non-monetary purpose, and what they would do next. Disclose that the evaluation was requested. Compare findings across multiple readers before inferring any improvement.

**Principle:** Respond to critical feedback visibly while keeping its origins, limits, and unresolved questions attached.
