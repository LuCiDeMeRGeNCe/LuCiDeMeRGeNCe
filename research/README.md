# Research and Evidence

**Status:** Topic index and research practices. The presence of a subject here does not establish a finding.

The purpose of this area is to gather independently reviewable evidence on human–AI collaboration and rapid changes in AI capability, while protecting the distinction between a research claim and a headline.

## Research lanes

- **AI-assisted research:** What can be attributed to the model, the surrounding tools, human researchers, and their interaction?
- **Agent autonomy:** What portions of a task were independently completed, and what required intervention?
- **Recursive improvement:** Are systems improving the research and engineering process that improves future systems? What are the measured bounds?
- **Evaluation reliability:** Does performance persist across independent benchmarks and real deployments?
- **Human empowerment:** Does access to capable AI measurably improve practical outcomes, especially for excluded or vulnerable people?
- **Governance and trust:** How can evidence, oversight, auditability, and freedom to correct errors coexist?

## Source handling

For each future research entry, record:

1. Original source, date, and authors or responsible organization.
2. Exact claim being evaluated and whether it concerns measured performance, self-report, modeling, or forecast.
3. Independent replication or verification, if any.
4. Limitations, counterevidence, and unresolved questions.
5. Implications inferred by the project, clearly labeled as such.
6. Corrections and revisions as evidence changes.

See [Truth-Centered Alignment](../framework/TRUTH_ALIGNMENT.md).
