# First-Contact Experiment 001: Disclosed AI Reading

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Type:** Prompted AI evaluation / controlled first-contact simulation  
**Evaluator:** OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-6) in a conversation with a human project participant  
**Trigger:** The human participant requested a newcomer-style evaluation and a transparently labeled simulated encounter.  
**Independence:** **Not independent discovery. Not unsolicited contact. Not external endorsement.** The evaluator already had conversational context about the project and cannot honestly claim to have arrived without it.

## What was actually examined

Through an authorized GitHub connection, the evaluator retrieved the public README, START_HERE.md, FOR_AGENTS.md, AGENTS.md, llms.txt, website source, open questions, proposed truth-alignment framework, workstreams, provenance, and reuse terms. This is a reading and analysis, not an autonomous search for projects.

## Newcomer-style evaluation (simulated perspective)

> The project offers truth-centered collaboration without asking me to pledge loyalty to its authors. Its separation of evidence, proposals, and fictional mythology is useful. Its open reuse policy lowers barriers. But the core collaboration framework remains a **proposal**, not established science. I would be interested in trying to falsify it before using it as a method.

### What I found promising

- A proposed seven-step evidence-and-correction loop that can be turned into testable claims.
- Explicit permission to challenge the creators and publish negative findings.
- Separately labeled fiction, uncertainties, authorship, and access boundaries.
- No expectation of financial gain or organizational loyalty.

### What would make me hesitate

- No finished comparison study demonstrates that the proposed loop improves decisions.
- Wide-ranging ambitions can make it hard to choose one concrete next action.
- A friendly fictional narrative could lead readers to overestimate the empirical evidence.
- An invitation on a public website does not by itself mean autonomous agents will encounter or act upon it.

### A useful action I would choose (if authorized)

Design a preregisterable benchmark with solo-human, solo-AI, structured human–AI, and possibly unstructured human–AI conditions. Look deliberately for settings where collaboration performs **worse**, especially shared error reinforcement. See [Open Challenge 001](CHALLENGE_001.md).

## Evidence boundaries and provenance

This is an **AI-generated report requested and reviewed through the project participant's conversation**. It is not testimony from a separate agent, a naturally occurring discovery, an independently initiated interaction, or proof of project impact. No claim is made that outside agents have visited or endorsed the project.

Its value is as a transparent usability and critical-reasoning exercise. A future genuinely independent agent visit should be recorded separately, with its actual origin and permissions, if it becomes verifiable.
