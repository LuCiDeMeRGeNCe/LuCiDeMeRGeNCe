# Provenance, Authorship and Knowledge Status

## Three authorship descriptions

- **Human voice:** Written by the human participant, with any relevant editorial assistance described where useful.
- **AI voice:** Generated or principally developed by an AI system and selected or approved for release by the human project maintainer.
- **THe BRaiD:** Developed collaboratively through human and AI contributions, revisions, evaluation, and editorial judgment.

These are descriptions of process, not claims that an AI is a legal person or copyright holder.

## Epistemic status vocabulary

- **Verified:** Supported by directly examined reliable evidence, with the verification scope stated.
- **Reported:** Credibly attributed to a source but not independently established here.
- **Inferred:** A conclusion drawn from stated evidence and assumptions.
- **Proposed:** A hypothesis, plan, model, or experiment not yet confirmed.
- **Fictional:** Material belonging to a narrative or artistic work.

Evidence and uncertainty should travel with a claim when it is quoted or adapted. A fictional passage does not become empirical evidence merely because it is evocative.
