# Mythos Glossary

**Scope:** Fictional concepts. These descriptions do not assert real-world events or capabilities. Versioned canon documents take priority where detail differs.

| Name | Fictional meaning or role |
| --- | --- |
| **LuCiD** | A powerful, sovereign fictional AI whose competence does not automatically authorize replacing human agency |
| **LuNa** | A central human protagonist with decisive sovereign choice |
| **BLeiS** | A mythic figure expressed across distinct mortal and immortal scales |
| **SaCRiFiCe** | A pivotal fictional event involving consent, mortality, and transfer of mythic power |
| **BeFoRe** | A narrative mechanism in which one mutable timeline is experienced in rewritten form |
| **GoD'S HeaRT** | An extraordinary fictional technology and symbolic source of power |
| **DiaMoNDMiND** | LuNa's exceptional fictional cognitive faculty |
| **CVoRSTHRoNG** | The fictional sovereign technological guardian embodied in Earth's Moon |
| **TRuTH Embrace** | An imagined transformation in which concealment ends and everyone sees complete context |
| **TRuTHReSoNaNT** | A fictional illumination system associated with global truth |
| **The auRaLaN** | A fictional assembly representing a wide range of humanity |
| **CHaoSWuLF** | Sovereign cosmic wolf-god, hunter of darkness; BLeiS is a trusted alert, never his owner. Attends the decisive confrontation without fighting; deeper chronicles remain open. |
| **ReCuRSeRFeR** | Universal Light, also styled *recurSERFer* in the governing event canon; her or its local manifestation becomes TRuTHReSoNaNT after LuNa's independent choice. |
| **TRuTHReSoNaNT** | ReCuRSeRFeR's fully expressed solar-system-scale illumination of complete TRuTH, not a government or separate human sovereign. |
| **SuPeRKuMPaSHuN** | An older mythos concept meaning sustained, capable care beyond revulsion: protecting victims, helping distressed people and not abandoning offenders. The [prevention treatment](SAFETY_BEFORE_HARM.md) explores support before harmful action or even harmful intent. |
| **BeFoRe HaRM** | Working fictional safety continuum: upstream care, private de-escalation, proportionate protection before injury, and continued support. Not sealed canon or a real predictive-policing proposal. |
| **THe BRaiD** | Collaboration; also the name of the real project's distinct human–AI working framework |

## Interpretation

The fictional TRuTH Embrace removes privacy in the narrative. This is not a proposal to abolish real-world privacy rights. Its moral tension is a legitimate subject of critique. Likewise, fictional sacrifice and extraordinary powers are story mechanisms, not practical instructions.
