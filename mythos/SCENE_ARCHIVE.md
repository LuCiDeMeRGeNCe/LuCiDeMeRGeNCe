## Two legitimate script architectures

### Eleven-movement treatment

The early skeleton and September 4 master preserve these eleven **major movements**:

1. **THe FiNaL BaTTLe:** LuNa rises, fights, fails to solve the underlying threat, Moon hurtles toward Earth; HeaRT FIReS; cut without rescue.
2. **FiND HeR / FiNDiNG LuNa:** the living history that makes the loss meaningful.
3. **THRouGH aND PaST DeaTH:** SaCRiFiCe, mutual authorization and the Heart transfer.
4. **BLeePCRaFT / aToMiC PaLM:** intimacy, humor and the original gesture.
5. **THe GLaRE:** new information about rage, strategy and LuNa's first devastating attack.
6. **THe SoNG KNeW:** BLeiS's song recognizes LuNa before she fully recognizes herself.
7. **He STaYS:** sacrifice, incarceration, fidelity and reunion with LuCiD.
8. **MouNTaiNBaCK / THe PRiNCe oF aLL LuPiNe:** the King, KaeL, withheld truth and a rewritten rescue.
9. **THe VeSSeL / BoRRoWeD TiMe:** the Heart's previous installation and entrusted delivery.
10. **GoD MoDe GoLD:** unseen battle angles and the limits of force.
11. **THe auRaLaN:** the human chorus; a **proposed** location for the after-cut aLL TRuTH solution.

This is a **treatment**, not eleven finished screenplay scenes. The proposal to place the withheld resolution in Scene 11 is not a locked decision. The older epilogue follows with recollection, BeFoRe and CoMiNG HoMe.

### September 23 working registry

A later creative pass changes **how developed scenes are counted**, not the reality of all previous work. The registry records only:

**Numbered and active:** Scene 01 THe FiNaL BaTTLe; Scene 02 FiNDiNG LuNa; Scene 03 SaCRiFiCe.

**Unnumbered:** THe TRuNKiNG; THe FLoWeR; BLeePCRaFT; WuLT; DeLeTeD uNReaD; and an **unresolved Moon / ReViVaL ending**. Several are explicitly *candidates*, not sealed scenes. Other eleven-movement ideas have not ceased to exist; they simply do not all hold numbered positions in that working pass.

This inventory preserves the **full work history without pretending a production order has been approved**.

## The after-cut sequence found in working material

The saved screenplay excerpts include an explicitly labeled **“LATER SEQUENCE — PLACEMENT UNASSIGNED”** following the opening's fade:

- Moon still coming; the solar power of the Heart refracts through DiaMoNDMiND.
- LuNa reaches aLL TRuTH; the auRaLaN recognize the answer.
- A proposed 693/107 split concerns *immediate enactment* of aLL TRuTH, not whether to initiate the already-ratified Moon containment plan.
- CVoRSTHRoNG stops himself. Working staging shows his mantle continuing outward and the technological inner body revealed.
- The implementation and its human-scale effects remain **unwritten on screen** in that saved script. Related prose describes universal care, practical abundance, accountable decisions and the ending of compulsory survival labor.

**This is the principal missing connective tissue of the published mythology's screen presentation:** we had the in-world explanation in governing prose, but no publicly linked story treatment explaining how the Moon cliffhanger was deliberately withheld and later answered. [THe aNSWeR](THE_ANSWER_ALL_TRUTH.md) makes that visible now.

## Recommended development status

- **SEALED CANON:** Existing versioned governing mythos documents, until explicit replacement or amendment.
- **RECOVERED STORY SPINE:** [THe aNSWeR — aLL TRuTH](THE_ANSWER_ALL_TRUTH.md), a non-governing bridge between existing events and purpose.
- **DRAFTABLE / WORKING:** Approved core of Scenes 01–03, source screenplay pages, later scene briefs.
- **CANDIDATE / UNNUMBERED:** Material whose placement and final form are still being developed.
- **OPEN THOUGHT EXPERIMENTS:** Total public revelation, comprehensive system knowledge with bounded disclosure, and alternative knowledge architectures. See [aLL TRuTH — Open Thought Experiments](ALL_TRUTH_THOUGHT_EXPERIMENTS.md). The author has not selected a final comprehensive truth model.
- **CONFLICT / DECISION NEEDED:** Alternate BLeiS ending and any unreconciled screenplay chronology. The privacy descriptions are **different historical models under deliberate exploration**, not an editorial tie we may decide implicitly.
- **PROTECTED MYSTERY:** A purposeful question left unanswered by the creative author; not equivalent to a missing draft.

**Archive policy:** Preserve the originals privately. Publish summaries, indexes and selected creative material **with explicit statuses and authorial provenance**. Do not paste entire old drafts, sensitive human life histories, third-party material or superseded claims onto the site without a deliberate editorial pass.
